Three recommended themes and how to adapt them for GIGW (India)

1) Dot-Org (Hugo)
- Demo / details: https://themes.gohugo.io/themes/dot-org-hugo-theme/
- Why: Designed for organisations and causes, content-first layout, easy to list reports and news.
- Adaptation notes:
  - Add skip-link and ensure `lang` attributes are set.
  - Replace theme header/footer with GIGW-required contact & legal blocks.
  - Test color contrast and add accessible focus states.

2) Newsroom / News-focused Hugo themes
- Demo / details: https://themes.gohugo.io/themes/newsroom/
- Why: Built for press releases, announcements and media galleries — good for achievement showcases.
- Adaptation notes:
  - Ensure PDFs and press releases include structured metadata.
  - Provide transcripts for multimedia and alt text for images.
  - Add language switcher and ensure accessible navigation for long lists.

3) Minimal Mistakes (Jekyll)
- Demo / details: https://mmistakes.github.io/minimal-mistakes/
- Why: Mature, well-documented Jekyll theme with good accessibility practices and flexible layouts.
- Adaptation notes:
  - Convert layouts to include GIGW header/footer, skip link and metadata.
  - Use the theme's collections for reports and ensure document downloads are accessible.

General adaptation checklist (short)
- Add skip link, `lang` attributes, and semantic headings.
- Ensure WCAG contrast, keyboard navigation, and ARIA labels.
- Host PDFs with accessible tagging and provide transcripts for audio/video.
- Run automated and manual accessibility tests before launch.

Links & resources
- GIGW / NIC guidance: follow `meity` / `nic` published policies and the `India Portal` approach for metadata and directories.
- MyGov and India Portal are good reference implementations for layout and content patterns.
